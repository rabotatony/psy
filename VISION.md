# PSYWEAVE — VISION & SPEC (THE TARGET)

This document is the product's north star. Every build decision is validated against it.
We are not building a web page. We are building the leading psychedelic music instrument —
software that happens to ship as an installable app.

---

## 1. North Star

A person who has never made music opens PSYWEAVE on their phone.
Within 10 minutes they are performing an original, genre-authentic psychedelic track —
full production quality, evolving arrangement, their own loops and gestures in it —
and they can keep playing for hours without repeating themselves.
The instrument sounds *better than the sum of its influences*: it borrows the grammar of
Astrix / Infected Mushroom / Vini Vici / Shpongle-era production and re-synthesizes it live.

## 2. Laws (unbreakable)

1. **No wrong notes.** Every exposed control is musical; generation is constrained to key, scale and genre grammar.
2. **One tap = full production.** PLAY always yields a complete, arranged, mixed track.
3. **Depth on demand.** The surface is simple (macros, stage buttons). Expert layers (sequencer, song chain, MIDI, stems) are available but never required.
4. **Infinite by generation, not by content.** No sample packs; synthesis + generative grammar = endless originality in a few hundred KB.
5. **Sound quality above feature count.** The master chain (sidechain, limiter, stereo grammar, loudness targets) is sacred.
6. **Performance-first UI.** Glanceable state, big touch targets, one-hand play on a phone, PERF mode for stage.
7. **Instrument, not DAW.** We remove decisions; we do not add workflows.

## 3. Zero-knowledge journey

    open app -> PLAY -> track builds itself -> nudge ENERGY/SPACE/COLOR ->
    RISE/DROP for drama -> record loops / sing over it -> EXPORT -> share

Every step optional. Every step reversible.

## 4. Genre matrix (the psychedelic spectrum)

Each style = tempo range + scale vocabulary + bass archetype + lead archetype + drum topology + FX grammar + arrangement grammar.

| Style | BPM | Bass | Lead | Drums | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full-On | 140-145 | rolling 16ths | supersaw / wavefolder anthems | 4-on-floor, offbeat hats | uplifting, bright |
| Progressive | 130-138 | offbeat pump | wide detuned lines, space | minimal, groove-first | hypnotic, open |
| Dark Psy | 140-150 | rolling, distorted | acid 303, FM stabs | tight, aggressive | minor/phrygian tension |
| Forest | 148-160 | rolling, hollow | FM blips, organic stabs | tribal percussion layer | organic, twisted |
| Hi-Tech | 165-180 | hyper rolls | glitch zaps, fast arp | dense, chaotic | cartoon energy |
| Goa | 135-142 | galloping | twin-delay saw leads | classic, claps | vintage euphoria |
| Psybient | 80-110 | sub pulses | granular textures, flutes | downtempo, shakers | cinematic, wide |

The instrument must morph between these continuously (style blending), not just switch presets.

## 5. Synthesis architecture (engines)

- **Kick**: layered synthesis — click transient + mid punch + sub body, per-style tuning, sidechain trigger.
- **Bass**: pluck+sub rolling engine; offbeat engine; 303-style engine (resonant ladder envelope, accents, glide); drive stage.
- **Leads**: supersaw stack; wavefolder engine; FM screamer; formant squelch; granular texturizer. All through Haas widener + tempo-synced ping-pong delay.
- **Pads**: detuned saw stacks with slow spectral motion; ducked under the groove.
- **Percussion**: modal-synthesis toms/congas, metallic hats, noise claps — humanized velocity & pan.
- **FX grammar**: risers, impacts, zaps, sweeps, fills — generated by the arrangement, not triggered randomly.
- **Master**: sidechain pump -> drive -> glue comp -> limiter -> soft clip; loudness target ~ -9 LUFS for psy styles; stereo width discipline (mono-safe low end).

## 6. Intelligence & automation model

- **Macro surface**: ENERGY (density/intensity), SPACE (depth), COLOR (timbral hue), CHAOS (variation probability). Four knobs that always sound good.
- **Arrangement grammar**: section types with entry/exit rules; tension curve auto-shaped over minutes; user can override with Song Mode.
- **Motif grammar**: call & response, chord-tone anchoring, resolution rules; MUTATE = controlled evolution, never randomness.
- **Never-same guarantee**: seeded generation; every session unique; projects reproducible from seed.
- **Style morph**: continuous interpolation between genre grammars (tempo, scale, bass archetype, FX density).

## 7. Performance UX

- Phone-first: thumb-reach stage buttons, PERF fullscreen mode, haptic pulses on the bar.
- Looper as band member: quantized, tempo-compensated, overdub-friendly, meters visible.
- MIDI: any controller maps to any macro (LEARN).
- Visual feedback = the music's nervous system (radial spectrum, beat rings), not decoration.

## 8. Platform strategy

1. **Now**: installable PWA (manifest + service worker), offline-capable, Web Audio.
2. **Next**: AudioWorklet DSP (sample-accurate custom oscillators/filters), WebGPU visuals.
3. **Later**: native wrapper (Capacitor) for stores; same code, same projects.
4. Projects are portable: JSON (seed+state) + WAV/STEMS.

## 9. Sound-quality bar

Reference productions: Astrix "Artcore", Infected Mushroom "IM the Supervisor",
Vini Vici live sets, Zenonesque groove records.
Measured, not vibes: loudness (LUFS), crest factor, stereo correlation, transient clarity —
validated on offline renders in CI-like checks.

## 10. Definition of done (THE EDGE)

A first-time user, on a phone, performs a 5-minute original psychedelic set,
exports it, and cannot believe a machine let them do that.
Then they come back tomorrow and it is a different instrument again.
